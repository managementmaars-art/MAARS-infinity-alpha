# Guide Audit

Review the following checks prior to making commits and summarize the key points for the article.

## Word length checks
- The article should not be less than 1500-2000 words. Specify its word length and if we are under limit.

## DO NOT start with or talk about your own personal background
- Keep personal stories of the author out of the articles such as how they ran into the issue or how they landed up solving this. If this is specified mention it.

## DO NOT teach fundamentals
- Don't teach fundamentals about a language or a framework unless the article is trying to teach the reader about this topic; rather specify the library or tool and how its most used to solve for x cases.

## Summarize what the guide will achieve and who the target reader is

## Explain the benefits and outcome of the guide

## Include a diagram showing how components fit together

## Not just how but also why
- Don't just show steps, explain the reasoning behind choices. For example: "Slack doesn't provide an official MCP server, so we will need to build our own custom one. We'll use FastAPI and FastMCP for this."

## Make the writing valuable
- If the article mentions the reader might be incorrect in thinking, specify those cases.
- Make statements that a reasonable person could strongly disagree with and specify those cases.
