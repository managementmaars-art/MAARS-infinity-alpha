# MAARS Command — 3-Minute Commercial · Higgsfield Production Brief (v3)

**Spine of the film:** **Two protagonists' transformation journey** intercut throughout. The features are the *proof* of how MAARS gets them from where they were to where they end up. Every automation scene cuts back to a protagonist seeing the result and progressing.

**The three journeys:**

- **Protagonist A — Jamal — "From Nothing":** No business idea. No business knowledge. Working a dead-end barista job, dreaming of more. Asks MAARS "what business should I start?" → **MAARS analyzes his skills, market gaps, and recommends a real business for him** ("CodeFuel — Meal Prep for Tech Workers"). Then MAARS launches it — content, branding, outreach, fulfillment workflows — all of it. By the end: first international sale, then scaling, then a real brand making him real money.
- **Protagonist B — David — "From Chaos":** Existing small-business owner (wholesale plumbing supply) drowning in calls, emails, invoices, contractor bills. Plugs MAARS in. **MAARS cuts his contractor costs by replacing them with named AI specialists, saves him 60+ hours/week through automation, and grows his revenue through cold outreach and campaigns running 24/7.** By the end: business runs itself, P&L flips from red to green, and David has his life back.
- **Protagonist C — Aisha — "From Passion to Brand":** Former fitness instructor who quit her studio gig — burned out, broke, but with deep expertise in women's wellness, training, and nutrition. Wants her own brand but has *no idea what product to sell*. Asks MAARS "I have wellness expertise — what should I build?" → **MAARS researches the women's wellness market, identifies the gap, and recommends "FORMA"** — a premium combined athleisure + clean supplement subscription brand for wellness-focused women 25–45. Then MAARS launches it: brand identity, product line, e-commerce store, paid ads, geo-translation, customer DMs, retail outreach — every step. By the end: revenue $84k/mo MRR, international shipping, two major retail accounts (Sephora for supplements + a national athleisure boutique chain), customer support handled while she sleeps.

**The three things the commercial must hammer:**
1. **MAARS makes you money** (revenue) — visible in growing $ counters and "first sale" / "47 new orders" notifications
2. **MAARS cuts your costs** (savings) — visible in P&L shifts, "Replaced 3 contractors → saved $4,800/mo" overlays
3. **MAARS saves you time** (hours back) — visible in "60 hours/week saved" overlays + the "empty office running itself" shots

**MAARS does the whole build — end-to-end:**
- **Research** the market and the opportunity (Dr. Clara Voss · Research Specialist)
- **Recommend the business idea** + positioning + pricing (Victor Ashford · Business Strategist · Cassandra Steele · Chief Strategy Officer · Maximilian Wolfe · Revenue Strategist)

- **Build the mobile app** — iOS + Android storefronts (Kai Nakamoto · App Developer)
- **Source products + suppliers** — manufacturing, fulfillment, vendors (Adrian Stone · Procurement & Vendor Manager)

- **Finance + compliance + legal** — books, contracts, audits, ToS (Benjamin Cole · Alexandra Reid · Victoria Harrington)
