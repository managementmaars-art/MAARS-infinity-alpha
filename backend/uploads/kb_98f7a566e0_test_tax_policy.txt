Bangladesh Income Tax Act 2025

Section 1: Overview
The Bangladesh Income Tax Act of 2025 establishes the framework for income taxation in Bangladesh. This act replaces the previous Income Tax Ordinance of 1984.

Section 2: Tax Rates for Individual Taxpayers
For the tax year 2025-2026, the following tax rates apply:
- First BDT 350,000: NIL (Tax-free threshold)
- Next BDT 100,000: 5%
- Next BDT 300,000: 10%
- Next BDT 400,000: 15%
- Next BDT 500,000: 20%
- Remaining amount: 25%

Section 3: Corporate Tax Rates
- Listed companies: 22.5%
- Non-listed public companies: 27.5%
- Other companies: 30%
- Banks and financial institutions: 37.5%
- Mobile phone operators: 40%

Section 4: VAT Registration
All businesses with an annual turnover exceeding BDT 3 crore must register for VAT. The standard VAT rate is 15%.

Section 5: Tax Filing Deadlines
- Individual taxpayers: September 30 of the assessment year
- Corporate taxpayers: By the 15th day of the 7th month following the year end
- Advance tax installments: Due on September 15, December 15, March 15, and June 15

Section 6: Penalties
- Late filing penalty: BDT 2,000 or 10% of tax liability, whichever is higher
- Tax evasion: Up to 100% of the evaded amount plus criminal prosecution
